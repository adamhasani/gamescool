Ridge Hunters brings an idle party RPG to Gamescool

Meet Ridge Hunters, an idle party RPG with a short prologue. Learn where your heroes go and how to steer them on keyboard or touch.

8 Oct 2026 · 1 min read · Gamescool

A party that fights by itself

Ridge Hunters is out on Gamescool. It is a single-player idle party RPG in which four heroes fight automatically while you steer with scrolls, tonics, chests and level-ups.

Your company sets out from the village gate, faces ambushes on the road and makes its way into a barrow. The party keeps fighting while you watch, so there is nothing to mash. Your part is to read a scroll when a fight turns, hand out a tonic and choose when a hero levels up.

This first release contains the prologue, which lasts a few minutes. Its route ends with the Barrow-Gnawer, the first boss, waiting in its crypt. More chapters are on the way.

How to steer your heroes

On a phone, you can play upright or sideways. Tap a hero when its + appears to level it up, or tap a scroll or tonic when a fight turns. When a chest waits, tap OPEN before its timer runs out.

Keyboard controls give you access to the same decisions, along with speed and zoom controls:

  • Press 1, 2 or 3 to use scrolls.
  • Press Q or W to use tonics.
  • Press Space to open a chest.
  • Press L to level up.
  • Press Tab to control speed.
  • Use the mouse wheel to zoom.

The heroes handle the fighting themselves throughout the route. You watch how the fight develops and use these controls to intervene.

A short first run with a boss at the end

The prologue gives you a few minutes of road ambushes, barrow fights and a boss encounter. Automatic combat leaves you free to watch the party, while timed chests and choices about scrolls, tonics and level-ups give you things to act on.

You do not need to sign in to play. Your company’s progress saves on the device you use.
