Wayang Rush tips for parries, counters and stamina

Learn Wayang Rush's parry timing, counter rhythm, stamina management and heavy slash openings, plus how to handle each phase.

30 Sep 2026 · 4 min read · Gamescool

Play Wayang Rush with parries as your main way to open the duelist's guard. Plain attacks get blocked while he guards, so your damage depends on earning counter-slashes, using recovery windows and choosing when to charge a heavy.

Choose your mode and learn the main controls

Start on Normal while you learn the strike timings. It gives you wider parry windows, softer incoming hits and a duelist with 300 health; Hard gives him 400 health and tighter timing.

  • Move with WASD or the arrow keys.
  • Press J or left-click to attack; hold for a heavy slash.
  • Tap K, right-click or Shift to parry; hold to block.
  • Press Space to dash and L or F to use your skill when focus is full.

On touch, use the left-side stick and the Attack, Parry, Dash and Skill buttons on the right. Press O to open Options and pause; changing Mode there starts a new duel.

Time your parry near contact

Watch the blue glint on his blade, but don't treat its first appearance as the perfect-parry cue. On Normal, the ordinary glint begins 22 frames before contact, while a perfect parry needs your press within the last 9 frames. On Hard, those timings shrink to 10 frames and the last 6 frames.

Pressing earlier can earn a normal parry instead. Repeated tapping is unreliable: another press within 0.25 s on Normal or 0.30 s on Hard opens no parry window.

When unsure, keep the button held. After 0.20 s, holding blocks, but you still take 5% of the strike's damage on Normal or 10% on Hard, and blocking gives no counter.

A perfect parry raises the gold gunungan shield for 0.8 s. Stay put if another blockable strike is coming quickly: strikes into the shield do no damage. Moving more than 0.6 m away, attacking or dashing ends that protection.

Turn parries into deliberate counter-slashes

A normal parry opens one counter; a perfect parry opens two. Press J within 1.1 s on Normal or 0.75 s on Hard to take the opening, then time the second press instead of immediately tapping twice.

  • On Normal, press again 0.15 to 0.90 s after your first counter press.
  • On Hard, press again 0.25 to 0.60 s after the first.
  • An early second press is ignored, so press again within the valid window.

The first counter deals 22 damage; the second deals 33 and displays CRIT. Both bypass his guard and make you invincible during the counter, and a landed counter ends his current string.

A perfect parry followed by both landed counters deals 55 damage and earns 30% focus. Only the first counter costs stamina, and an earned counter remains available even if your parry emptied the bar.

Save stamina and use perfect dashes

Watch the thin white stamina bar beneath your health. On Hard, a parry and its first counter cost 17%, and regeneration waits 0.9 s after your last spend; on Normal, that delay is 0.6 s.

Holding block also drains stamina with each blocked strike: 6% on Normal and 10% on Hard. Treat it as a fallback while learning, because repeated blocks still cost health and stamina.

At 0%, LOW STAMINA appears and stays until you recover above 15%. While it shows, parry presses open no window, and dash and heavy slash are refused. Blocking a strike at 0% causes GUARD BREAK, full damage and a stagger.

Use perfect dashes before you reach that state. Press Space just before a strike that would hit you lands; with no direction held, you dash straight back from the duelist.

  • Normal perfect-dash timing: start 0.03 to 0.25 s before contact.
  • Hard perfect-dash timing: start 0.05 to 0.15 s before contact.

A perfect dash refunds 25% stamina, adds 20% focus and opens one counter. Moving away still qualifies if the strike would have hit your starting spot.

Charge heavies into useful openings

Hold J through the initial swing to charge. The charge fills 0.5 s after your press, marked by a gold pop on the blade; release then to leap into the heavy slash. Releasing early spends nothing and produces no heavy.

Aim for his recovery immediately after a string. A heavy there deals 24 damage and staggers him for 1.0 s, while a heavy into his guard deals 8 damage and breaks it for a 1.2 s stagger.

Don't repeat the guard-breaking heavy immediately. For 5 s on Normal or 8 s on Hard afterward, another heavy into his guard is blocked and triggers an immediate strike back.

Charging slows your walk to 30% speed, and a hit cancels it. Charging within 4.4 m while he circles also provokes his next string, so don't assume that pause is safe. Use plain attacks during recovery or stagger, when his guard cannot block them.

Adapt to the later phases and spend focus

Watch the marks on his health bar. Below 60%, he adds overheads, spins and lightning slashes; the spin hits twice, 0.4 s apart, so defend against both contacts. Lightning can be parried, but its glint is shorter.

Below 30%, watch for these warnings:

  • Crimson diamond: the unblockable strike cannot be parried or blocked. Dash or stay beyond its 3.9 m reach.
  • Filling ground ring: the burst cannot be parried or blocked. Dash, or move outside its 3.2 m circle.

When all three focus pips light up, press L or F for the crimson flurry. It deals 68 damage, cannot miss, makes you invincible for 1.85 s and ends his string. Use it to interrupt pressure rather than continuing to spend stamina defending with full focus.
