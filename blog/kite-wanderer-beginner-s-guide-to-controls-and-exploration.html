Kite Wanderer beginner's guide to controls and exploration

Learn Kite Wanderer's keyboard, controller and phone controls, navigate the opening cave, and manage stamina, combat and saves.

9 Oct 2026 · 4 min read · Gamescool

An island to explore at your own pace

Kite Wanderer is a single-player open-world adventure on a volcanic island. You climb cliffs, glide on a bamboo kite and search for twelve hidden Ketupat among ruins, forests, rice terraces and the volcano.

It suits you if you enjoy exploring landmarks and finding collectibles alongside combat. You have a golok for fighting, four powers to earn through trials and a Journal that names your next goal. After the opening whisper, there are no people or dialogue on the island.

Learn the controls for your device

The opening cave introduces movement and interaction before combat and powers become available. You can also find a controls card for your device in the pause menu's System tab.

Keyboard and mouse

  • Move with WASD or the arrow keys; look with the mouse. The first click captures the mouse; if capture is refused, hold the right mouse button and drag.
  • Press Space to jump, press it again in the air to open the kite, and again to fold it.
  • Hold Shift to sprint. With your golok out or a target locked on, it dodges instead.
  • Left-click to attack; hold attack after a swing to charge a spin.
  • Press E to use things or let go of a wall. C also lets go.
  • Press Q to lock on. Hold right-click or F to aim, then tap left-click for a small power shot or hold it to charge an Art.
  • Use 1-4 to select powers, scroll the mouse wheel to cycle them, or hold Tab for the power wheel.
  • Press M for the map and Esc or P to pause.

Controller

  • Use the left stick to move and the right stick to look.
  • Press A to jump or glide, B to sprint or dodge, X to attack and Y to use things or let go.
  • Use LB to lock on and LT to aim.
  • Tap RB for the next power or hold it for the power wheel.
  • Press View for the map and Start to pause.

Phone

A movement stick appears under your left thumb, while the right side controls your view. Settings lets you change Touch look from Stick to Swipe and adjust sensitivity.

  • The bottom-right arc holds Jump, Attack, Sprint, Use, Lock and Art.
  • Jump changes to Glide, Drop or Climb as needed; Use becomes Let go while climbing.
  • Hold Attack to charge, hold Sprint to sprint, and hold Art to charge before releasing to cast.
  • Tap the power chip to switch powers or hold it for the wheel.
  • Map and Pause sit at the top right.

Leave the cave and climb the tower

Choose New Game to wake in The Waking Cave without your equipment. Find the Kite, Batik shirt, Rolled trousers and Golok; the optional Batik headband sits in a small chest in the west alcove. Clothing pickups let you choose Wear it or Later.

At the stone door, use Raise the kite to open the way outside. Your first goal is to climb the Lookout Tower, where standing on the deck fills in the map with a watercolour reveal.

The tower is about 36 m tall and has rest ledges. Stop on those ledges to refill stamina instead of trying to climb the whole height at once. There is also a Ketupat on the lookout deck.

Be ready before collecting the Ketupat under the banyan near the starting meadow. Picking it up triggers an ambush by three grunts.

Keep stamina for the trip back down

A full stamina wheel gives you 5.5 s of sprinting, 11 s of climbing or 25 s of gliding. It does not refill while you climb or glide, so reach somewhere you can stand before it empties.

On the ground, recovery begins after 0.6 s without stamina use. If you empty the wheel, you become exhausted until it fills completely: you cannot sprint, climb or glide, and movement drops to 60% speed.

Open the kite while airborne at least 2 m above the ground. You can glide into a cliff to grab it, but overhangs cannot be climbed.

Falling never costs hearts, though a hard landing causes a crouch recovery or a roll if you keep pushing forward. Water is different: there is no swimming, and water deeper than 1.2 m returns you to your last safe spot.

Start with the golok, then earn powers

You begin with three hearts. Lock onto a foe and use your golok's three-hit combo; before the world toughens, a full combo defeats a normal Raider grunt.

Dodge just before a blow lands to slow time, then attack promptly to start a Flurry rush. On keyboard, use Shift to dodge with your golok out or while locked on, then left-click to attack.

Fire, Ice, Wind and Growth remain locked until you win their respective plateau trials. Each trial has three waves, and dying or moving 32 m away fails it. Major victories also raise the world tier, making ordinary foes tougher as you progress.

Check your Journal and saves

Open the Journal when you need direction; it tracks your discoveries and names the next goal. The Creatures tab records enemies you have met and their weaknesses.

New Game works as a guest, and progress saves in this browser, with position saved every 20 s of play. For account saves, sign in from the title menu; Continue and Load Game require sign-in.

Your account holds one journey, one manual save and five rotating autosaves. Use Pause, System, Save for a manual save, or choose Bring it to bring a journey from this device to your account.
