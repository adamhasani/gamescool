Chopper Rush beginner's guide to takeoff and landing

Learn Chopper Rush's keyboard, controller and phone controls, start the turbine, practise hovering and make your first paid landing.

7 Oct 2026 · 4 min read · Gamescool

Choose your first flight

Play Chopper Rush to fly a helicopter over Jakarta at dusk, with real map data, Sudirman towers and rooftop landing spots. This free, single-player browser flight sim suits you if you want to learn helicopter controls while exploring and scoring clean landings. There are no flight enemies; buildings, water and rough touchdowns are the hazards.

Choose Fly on the title screen, then select a helipad. All 56 pads are available immediately, including the ground-level Monas park pad; Menara Astra roof is the default start.

Real is the default flight model. For a simpler start, choose Easy under Settings, Flight model; it applies from your next flight. Easy starts the engine automatically, holds height and brakes to a hover when you release the controls.

Learn the controls for your device

In Real, the collective is a lever that stays where you leave it. The cyclic tilts the helicopter, while the pedals turn its nose. Learn those separately before combining them.

Keyboard

  • I: start or stop the engine. T: switch throttle between idle and flight.
  • Space or Up arrow: raise collective. Shift or Down arrow: lower it.
  • W / S: tilt forward / back. A / D: tilt left / right.
  • Q / E or Left arrow / Right arrow: pedals.
  • Z: toggle SAS. X: toggle HOLD, the autopilot hover.
  • V: chase or cockpit camera. Drag the mouse to look around.
  • L: lights. M: map. Tab or H: Guide. Esc: pause.

Controller

  • Left stick: cyclic. RT / LT: collective up / down while held.
  • LB / RB: pedals. Right stick: look around.
  • Hold X for 0.5 s: starter. D-pad down: throttle.
  • D-pad right: SAS. A: HOLD and menu confirm. B: back.
  • Y: lights. Right-stick click: camera.
  • D-pad up: map. D-pad left: Guide. Menu: pause.

These are the standard controller mappings. On-screen button names follow the kind of controller connected.

Phone

Turn your phone sideways. The left FLY stick controls cyclic; drag the right-edge LEVER to set collective, and use the right PEDALS stick to turn the nose. The lever stays where you leave it.

Tap ENGINE to start or stop, and use THR for throttle. SAS is also on the left; THR and SAS hide once you are flying. HOLD, CAM and MAP sit on the right, with pause and the ? Guide button at the top left; drag empty screen to look.

In Easy, the touch right stick controls up / down and turning. X, controller A or the ASSIST button toggles hover assist.

Start the turbine and find a hover

Keep flight school enabled for your first attempt. Real has nine steps, taking you from engine start through hovering, forward flight and landing. Open the Guide whenever you need the checklist or instrument explanations: the flight pauses while you read.

  • Start with collective down. Press I and watch ENGINE settle near 65% at idle, roughly 12.5 s after starting.
  • Set flight throttle. Press T; with the lever down, ROTOR takes about 4 s to reach 100%.
  • Get light on the skids. Tap Space to raise collective in small steps. The school targets about 40% POWER here.
  • Keep the nose straight. More power swings it right, so correct with the pedals. Tap rather than holding: a pedal key reaches full input in about 0.6 s.
  • Practise hovering. The school asks for a steady hover under 50 FT for 2 s. Press X if you need HOLD to maintain hover, height and heading.

SAS starts on and damps rotation. Once you can hover, use W to tilt forward and follow the school's Fly lesson.

Read the instruments before landing

Use HEIGHT to judge clearance above whatever is underneath you. ALTITUDE measures height above the sea, so it is a different reading over a rooftop. CLIMB tells you how quickly you are rising or sinking.

Watch ROTOR and POWER as you fly. ROTOR should be 100%; below 95% in the air, you sink. Keep POWER under 100 and HEAT under 775.

For a paid Real-model landing, settle the skids with ground speed under 3 m/s, tilt under 12 degrees and descent softer than 300 FPM. Ease down with collective while watching CLIMB. A sink of 2.0 m/s or more causes a hard landing with damage and no points; over 4.5 m/s crashes.

Make your first landings count

Air time and distance earn points, but rooftops and helipads pay more. Plan a short hop to another building before attempting landmark moves.

  • Ground landing: 10 points.
  • Roof: 100 on your first landing on that building during a flight, then 50.
  • Helipad: 75; a new roof with a pad pays 100.
  • Jakarta First: 250 the first time you complete it.

A landing must be at least 150 m in a straight line from your start or last paid landing to pay. Monuments and protected buildings award no landing points. For a later target, Rooftop run asks you to land on five different roofs in one flight.

Know what persists and what Street changes

One crash ends your flight, but earned points stay banked. Rotor strikes and water also end flights, so leave clearance around obstacles. Turn back when the map-edge warning appears.

Your callsign, best flight, totals, Jakarta Firsts and flight-school progress save on this device. Each flight is scored separately, with no unlocks or levels. Offline leaderboard points stay on the device.

The title's Street button starts you on foot on Jalan Medan Merdeka Selatan, near a scooter and sedan. Its controls differ: use WASD to move and F to enter or leave a vehicle; on controller, use the left stick and Y. On phone, use the floating left stick and ENTER when near a vehicle; Street saves money, discoveries, kopi tokens and stats.
