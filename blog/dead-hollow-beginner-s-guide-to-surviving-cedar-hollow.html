Dead Hollow beginner's guide to surviving Cedar Hollow

Learn Dead Hollow's keyboard, controller and phone controls, choose your starting mode, and follow the opening route to a safehouse.

6 Oct 2026 · 4 min read · Gamescool

What to expect in Cedar Hollow

Play Dead Hollow for a single-player, 2D side-view survival RPG about finding your sister Maya and reaching the last train out. You walk left and right through Cedar Hollow, enter buildings, search containers and follow quests across three streets and a rail yard.

It suits you if you enjoy searching rooms, managing supplies and deciding when to fight or run. Guns make more noise than melee weapons, so choosing a weapon also means choosing how much attention to attract.

Choose your mode and occupation

For your first run, Story lets you learn the streets without hunger or rest needs. Ammo and medicine are easier to find, and food heals you. Survival requires you to eat and rest, makes ammo scarce and removes healing from food.

Leave the optional challenge toggles off while learning. Headshots only reduces anything other than a head hit to 12% damage. One life deletes the save when you die, though it also gives an extra item per container and fewer Worn weapons.

Your occupation changes your starting skills and sometimes your equipment or attributes. Mechanic is a practical opening choice: you start with a Crowbar and Lockpicking at 26. That clears the 25 requirement for 18 Maple Row, where a cabinet holds a .38 Revolver and 12 rounds.

Learn the controls before moving on

Keyboard and mouse

Use the mouse to aim and the movement keys to position yourself. Keep shove and interact within reach: they handle escaping grabs and most actions around town.

  • Walk with A / D or Left / Right; hold Shift to run.
  • Left click to shoot or swing. Press Space or right click to shove.
  • Press R to reload. Use Q, 1, 2, 3 or the mouse wheel to switch weapons.
  • Press E to search, open, talk, travel or advance dialogue. Press T to take everything in a loot panel.
  • Press F for the flashlight and H to quick heal.
  • Press I, Tab or B for your backpack; C for character, J for journal and M for map.
  • Press G to help Maya up or swap guns with her. Press Esc to pause.

Controller

Move with the left stick and click it to run; aim with the right stick. Push the stick left and right to break a grab.

  • RT fires; LT or LB shoves.
  • X reloads, A interacts and Y switches weapons.
  • D-pad up controls the flashlight, down heals, left opens the map and right handles Maya.
  • Back opens the backpack; Start pauses.

Phone

Turn your phone sideways and walk with the left thumb stick; push past 80% to run. Drag on the right half to aim, and your aim stays where you left it when you lift your finger.

Use Shove, Use, Fire, Reload and Swap; hold Fire to keep attacking. Use shows the action it will perform, such as Search. Light, Heal and Help sit at the top, and aim assist is on by default for touch and controller.

Follow the opening route

You start beside your crashed car on Route 9, almost at midnight. Search before heading into town: the trunk supplies your first weapon, light, medicine and house key.

  • Wrong Turn: Search the trunk for a Tire Iron, a Flashlight, two Bandages, Bottled Water and the House Key for 14 Maple Row. Walk east into Cedar Hollow.
  • Home: Enter 14 Maple Row and search the bookshelf for Maya's note. This house also gives you a safehouse with a bed and footlocker.
  • The Neighbour: Visit Mr. Okafor at 22 Maple Row. He needs the car battery from the garage at number 9 so his truck can winch the bus away.

Searching takes a moment, so account for nearby zombies before opening a container. Its contents are rolled once per save, and anything you leave stays there. You can return for supplies instead of carrying everything immediately.

Fight without exhausting yourself

Use melee when you want to limit noise. A melee hit makes noise within 4 m, while gunshots alert zombies within 30-50 m and also make street zombies arrive faster and in larger groups. Aim for the head when shooting: headshots deal 2.5x damage.

Keep at least 11 stamina available for a shove. Sprinting and swinging both drain stamina, so stop spending it before you need an escape. If grabbed on keyboard, tap A and D alternately 7 times or press Space to shove.

Switch the flashlight off with F when you do not need it, because it draws zombies from 12 m. Press H when hurt to let quick heal choose an item based on your injuries. Watch for the horde warning: indoors, hordes can enter through a door.

Use safehouses and protect your progress

Return to 14 Maple Row to sleep and store equipment. Its bed restores 12% of your maximum health per hour and refills stamina, while the shared footlocker holds 40 kg. Nothing spawns in safehouses, and a due horde waits.

Drop or store excess weight before travelling, because exceeding your carry limit stops you moving. Ammo, cash, keys, notes and quest items weigh nothing. Cleared streets populate with zombies again when you enter, so returning through familiar ground still needs care.

Your progress uses three save slots in this browser, with automatic saves at events including entering areas, changing rooms and finishing conversations. You can also save through the pause menu, but an alerted zombie within 12 m prevents it. Save before a major fight; Continue on the title screen loads your newest slot.
