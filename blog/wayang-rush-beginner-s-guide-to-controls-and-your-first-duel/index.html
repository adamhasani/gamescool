Wayang Rush beginner’s guide to controls and your first duel

Learn Wayang Rush’s keyboard, controller and phone controls, choose a mode, and prepare for your first duel in the temple.

4 Oct 2026 · 4 min read · Gamescool

Start with Normal mode

Play Wayang Rush for a free, single-player sword duel in your browser. You face one duelist on a Nusantara temple terrace at night. It suits quick sessions and players who want to practise timing: he blocks your plain attacks, so parries create your main openings.

Choose Normal for your first run. Its wider timing, softer hits and shorter attack strings give you more room to learn; the duelist has 300 health instead of Hard’s 400. Each run is one duel, with three phases as his health falls.

On the first page, choose Normal or Hard with the arrow keys, A / D, or the controller D-pad, then press Enter or Start. On a phone, tap a mode to begin.

Learn your controls

Keyboard and mouse

Movement follows the camera: up moves away from it. The camera stays locked on the duelist, and temporary looks spring back.

  • Move: W, A, S, D or the arrow keys.
  • Attack: J or left-click. Hold for a heavy slash.
  • Parry: K, right-click or Shift. Tap to parry; hold to block.
  • Dash: Space. With no direction held, you dash straight back, away from the duelist.
  • Skill: L or F, when focus is full.
  • Camera: Q / E, or move the mouse after clicking the game.
  • Options: O.
  • Retry: R after the duel ends and the retry delay passes.

Controller

On a standard controller layout, use the left stick to move and the right stick to look around. The control hints at the bottom left change to match your last input device.

  • Attack: X; hold for heavy.
  • Parry: LB or RB; hold to block.
  • Dash: A.
  • Skill: Y.
  • Options: Select / Back, or Start during the duel.
  • Start or retry: Start on the first page or end card.

Phone

Place your thumb on the left half of the screen to bring up the floating movement stick. Attack, Parry, Dash and Skill sit at the bottom right; hold Attack for heavy or Parry to block. Skill stays dim until focus is full.

Drag on the right side, away from the buttons, to look around. The pause button at the top right opens Options, and tapping the end card retries after the delay.

Make your first opening

For your first exchange, watch the duelist approach and look for the blue glint on his blade. He attacks in strings, so defending against one strike does not necessarily finish his attack. Aim to tap Parry just before contact, rather than immediately when the glint appears.

A PERFECT PARRY needs contact within 0.15 s of your press on Normal, or 0.10 s on Hard. It raises a gold gunungan shield and opens two counter-slashes. A normal PARRY opens one.

Press Attack after the parry to counter. On Normal, your first press must come within 1.1 s; after a perfect parry, press again 0.15 to 0.90 s after the first press for the second counter. An earlier second press is ignored, so leave a beat between presses.

A counter that lands staggers the duelist and ends his current string. If you are unsure of your parry timing, keep Parry held to block instead. Blocking still lets some damage through and gives no counter, so use it as a fallback while learning.

Watch stamina and use your other attacks

Your health, thin stamina bar and three focus pips sit at the top left. Parries, counters, blocks, dashes and heavy slashes spend stamina. A perfect dash restores 25% stamina and opens one counter-slash.

When LOW STAMINA appears, stop repeatedly pressing Parry: it cannot open a parry while that state lasts. Dash and heavy slash are also refused, and blocking at 0% stamina causes a guard break. Allow stamina to recover; the warning clears once it rises above 15%.

For a heavy slash, hold Attack until the charge fills at 0.5 s, then release. Look for the gold pop on the blade, or the filling gold ring on the phone’s Attack button. Releasing early spends nothing and produces no heavy slash.

A charged heavy can break his guard, but repeating that guard break has a cooldown of 5 s on Normal and 8 s on Hard. During that cooldown, another heavy into his guard is blocked and he strikes back immediately. His recovery after a string is another opening for a heavy.

When all three focus pips light up, press Skill for the crimson flurry. It deals 68 damage, makes you invincible during the attack and ends his string.

Recognise the later phases

Watch the marks on the duelist’s health bar. Below 60%, he adds overheads, spins and lightning slashes. The spin hits twice, and lightning can still be parried despite its shorter warning glint.

Below 30%, watch for these attacks:

  • Crimson diamond above his head: an unblockable strike. Dash or stay out of reach; parrying and blocking fail.
  • Filling ring on the ground: a lightning burst around him. Dash to avoid it; it also cannot be parried or blocked.

Pause, adjust and retry

Open Options to pause the duel and adjust Flashes, Screen shake, Brightness or audio settings. You can also change Mode there, but doing so starts a new duel. Your mode choice and Comfort settings are remembered in this browser.

No progress or score is saved. After victory or defeat, wait 1.5 s, then press R, press controller Start, or tap the end card to fight again.
