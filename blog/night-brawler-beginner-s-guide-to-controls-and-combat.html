Night Brawler beginner's guide to controls and combat

Learn Night Brawler's keyboard, controller and phone controls, then practise combos, perfect dodges and Biscuit's tackle.

10 Oct 2026 · 5 min read · Gamescool

Start with Dex and Biscuit

Play Night Brawler for a free, single-player street brawler about Dex and his dog, Biscuit, taking back four street corners. You fight through a neon city after midnight, following a gang that is stealing the district's signs. It suits you if you want a story between fights and combat built around punches, launches and timed dodges.

The route runs through ZAPDOME ALLEY, NITECAP DINER, NEON ST STATION and THE ROOF. Each chapter has story scenes around its fights, with a city map between chapters. Mr. Blackout waits on the roof.

Choose STORY to begin at the alley. On keyboard, use the arrows or W A S D to highlight a menu item, then press Enter, Space or J to choose it.

Set up your controls

Movement follows the screen: up takes you into the picture, and right moves you toward screen-right. Learn where Punch, Kick and Dodge sit before trying an air combo.

Keyboard and mouse

  • Move: W A S D or the arrow keys.
  • Punch: J, Z or the left mouse button.
  • Kick: K, X or the right mouse button.
  • Jump: Space or C.
  • Dodge: L or either Shift key.
  • Dog: I, E or V.
  • Pause: Esc or P.

Controller

Use the left stick or d-pad to move. On the standard layout, press X to punch, Y to kick, A to jump and B to dodge. Send Biscuit with LB, RB or the right trigger, and pause with Start.

In menus, move with the stick or d-pad, choose with A and go back with B. Start opens or closes pause.

Phone

Hold your phone sideways for the best view, though upright play also works. Put your left thumb on the left half of the screen to bring up the floating movement stick. The right side has PUNCH, KICK, JUMP, DODGE and DOG, with pause in the top-right corner.

The DOG button shows three charge pips and greys out below one charge. Touch controls appear on your first touch and hide when you press a keyboard key.

Learn a ground string in the alley

Dex starts with 160 HP, and the alley's first wave contains three punks. Move close to an enemy and press J four times to perform a jab, cross, hook and spinning kick. The complete string deals 36 damage, and its final kick sends enemies flying.

Press K on its own for a heavy front kick that knocks an enemy away and breaks guard. Pressing K after one to three punches instead produces an uppercut that launches the target. Once you are comfortable with the ground string, try this sequence:

  • Press J for one to three punches, then K to launch.
  • Press Space immediately after the uppercut to chase the enemy upward.
  • Press J three times in the air to keep the enemy up.
  • Press K to slam the enemy into the ground.

You can slam at any point during the air string. Of Dex's own attacks, only the slam hits an enemy already lying on the ground, and none hit an enemy getting up. Avoid spending punches on a target that cannot take them.

Time your dodge and send Biscuit

Enemy attacks have warning bursts above their heads: yellow for punks and skaters, red for bruisers and the bosses. In the first two waves, only one enemy attacks at a time; from the third wave, two can attack together. Watch the attackers while the others circle.

Press L or Shift while holding a direction to dash that way; without a direction, Dex dodges backward. For a perfect dodge, an incoming strike that would reach you must land within 0.27 seconds of your press. Against a punk, avoid dodging immediately when its warning appears.

A perfect dodge slows time and gives Biscuit one charge. Press Punch or Kick within one second afterward to throw a flying knee that deals 20 damage and breaks guard. This is your chance to turn a successful dodge into an attack.

Press I or E with at least one dog charge to send Biscuit tackling a target. His tackle deals 12 damage, knocks the enemy down and breaks guard; if nobody is in range, he barks and keeps the charge. You start with one charge, can hold three, and also gain charge from KOs.

Handle guards, bosses and the station

A BRUISER blocks attacks from the front while standing or walking. Use a standalone K to break his guard immediately, or attack from his side or back. During his wind-up, use heavy attacks or Biscuit because light and medium hits only chip him.

Brick and Mr. Blackout are too heavy to launch or juggle. Use a standalone Kick, a counter knee or Biscuit's tackle to interrupt their wind-ups and knock them down. When a red charge lane appears, step sideways out of it; hitting the ring edge leaves the boss dazed for about 1.8 seconds.

On the roof, move out of the red circle marking Mr. Blackout's ground pound. At NEON ST STATION, stay away from the far platform edge when it warns of a train. The passing train deals 18 damage to Dex and 40 to enemies touching that edge.

Know what carries into the next chapter

Between chapters, your remaining health carries over with 40 HP added, while Marge's pie after the diner restores it completely. Biscuit's charges carry over too. Protecting your health in the alley and station helps you enter the next fight with more available.

Clearing a chapter unlocks the next corner in CHAPTERS, and cleared chapters are saved in this browser without an account save. Choosing an unlocked chapter starts you there with full health and one dog charge, then continues toward the ending.

If you lose, RETRY CHAPTER restores the health and charges you had when that chapter began. Press Esc or P to pause and revisit CONTROLS whenever you need a reminder.
