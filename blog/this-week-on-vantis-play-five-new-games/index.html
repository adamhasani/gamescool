This week on Gamescool: five new games

Catch up on five new games, three updates and the week's most played games, with play counts and hours from Player stats.

5 Oct 2026 · 2 min read · Gamescool

Kite Wanderer led this week's plays as five new games arrived on Gamescool. The releases cover island exploration, zombie survival, sword duels, street fights and demolition.

Player stats recorded 1846 plays and 61.7 hours for 2026-09-28 to 2026-10-05. That reporting window covers eight days, and the hours are rounded.

The most played games

Kite Wanderer heads the supplied leaders in both plays and hours. Here are those games ordered by play count:

  • Kite Wanderer: 511 plays, 21.2 hours.
  • Wayang: 312 plays, 7.5 hours.
  • Spartan Unbound: 192 plays, 4.4 hours.
  • Chopper Rush: 177 plays, 6.8 hours.
  • Meme Surge: 65 plays, 6.4 hours.

Five new games to play

  • Wayang Rush puts you in a sword duel under the moon at a Nusantara temple. Press K just before a strike lands to parry; a perfect parry opens two counter-slashes, while a normal parry opens one.
  • Dead Hollow sends you through Cedar Hollow to find Maya before the last train out. Search houses with E, choose loud guns or quiet bats, and press Space to shove.
  • Kite Wanderer gives you a volcanic island to explore with climbing and a bamboo kite. Climb the tower to fill in your map, then search for the twelve hidden Ketupat.
  • Night Brawler follows a fighter and his dog across four street corners, with scenes between fights. Dodge just as a hit lands to slow time and charge the dog's tackle.
  • Site Wrecker turns a website address into a demolition level, with loose letters and breakable boxes. Hold Space to fly your jetpack and use the left mouse button to fire.

Three games updated

Chopper Rush added real helicopter controls using collective, cyclic and pedals. Flight school teaches those controls, alongside a new flight display, trees and clear helipads.

Meme Warrior added Story Mode with five chapters. The update brings goals, briefings, ranks and a wider camera.

Wayang received its second release. The fight now has a Nusantara temple arena, a mood and music for every phase, new moves and an opening.
