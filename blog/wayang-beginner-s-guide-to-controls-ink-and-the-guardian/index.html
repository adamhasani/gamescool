Wayang beginner's guide to controls, ink and the Guardian

Learn Wayang's keyboard and controller controls, how to keep damage from healing, and when to turn life into ink.

3 Oct 2026 · 4 min read · Gamescool

Before you enter the arena

Play Wayang for a free, single-player browser boss fight in an ink-painted Nusantara temple arena. You face one Guardian through three phases, using dodges, parries and blade attacks. Each phase repaints the arena and has its own music.

It suits you if you want a focused fight built around reading attacks and choosing when to strike. There is one difficulty, with no mode select, levels or unlocks. Every run covers the full three-phase fight, and no progress is saved.

Use a keyboard and mouse or a controller. There are no touch controls, so you cannot play using only your phone's touchscreen. Comfort settings are remembered in your browser.

Set up your controls

Movement is camera-relative: up moves away from the camera, and right moves right on screen. Start by learning movement, attack, dodge and guard before adding heavy attacks and ink.

  • WASD or arrow keys: move.
  • Space: tap to dodge; keep holding to sprint.
  • J or left mouse button: attack.
  • K or right mouse button: heavy attack.
  • L: use the ink flurry skill, which needs an ink seal.
  • Shift: press to parry; keep holding to block. Either Shift key works.
  • I: hold to turn a life seal into ink.
  • Q and E: turn the view left and right.
  • O: open comfort settings and pause the fight. Close them with O or Esc.
  • R: fight again after victory or defeat, once 1.5 seconds have passed.

Click the game to capture the mouse and look with it, or drag on the game to adjust the view. The camera keeps the Guardian in frame and turns toward it faster while it attacks.

On a controller with a standard layout, use these bindings:

  • Left stick: move; a half-pushed stick walks.
  • Right stick: camera.
  • A: dodge; hold to sprint.
  • X: attack.
  • Y: heavy attack.
  • B: ink skill.
  • LB: parry or hold to block.
  • RT: hold to convert life into ink.
  • L3: sprint.
  • Back: options.
  • Start: fight again.

Spend your first minutes reading attacks

If the WAYANG title card shows “Press any key,” press a key, click or press a controller button to start the opening with sound. The arena paints into view before play begins. The key hints at the bottom right fade after 10 seconds of play.

Begin by watching the floor: every Guardian attack marks its landing area with a circle, line or cone. In the first phase, you face the sweep, lunge and ring. Use these opening attacks to practise a simple response:

  • Face the Guardian and hold Shift to block the sweep or lunge without taking damage.
  • Move out of the ring when its circle appears. It targets where you stood when it appeared and cannot be blocked.
  • Press a direction with Space to dash. Press Space without a direction for a back-step, then press it again during that step for a backflip.

Attack during the Guardian's rest after a move, but stay ready to defend after your own burst. Repeated hits up close build pressure and can trigger an immediate counter. In the first phase, that counter is the sweep.

Make your damage stay

Your light attack string has four hits, with a finisher on the fourth. Keep pressing J to continue it; waiting too long between attacks resets the string. Pressing K changes the heavy attack according to your place in the combo: a launch after the first light hit, a spin slash after the second or third, and a leap slam from neutral or after the finisher.

Pale gold on the boss bar is recoverable damage. Light hits, thrusts and ink-skill hits leave this wash, which starts healing after a second without damage. Finish a light combo or land any heavy attack to make the accumulated wash permanent lost health.

Watch the three bronze gong pips beneath the boss bar too. Perfect parries, heavies and the ink skill damage them; light hits and thrusts do not. Breaking all three stuns the Guardian for a four-second sketch window, when every hit deals permanent damage.

Keep life before spending ink

Your five bottom-left seals show gold for life, indigo for ink and faded for empty. Hold I for 0.8 seconds to convert the rightmost life seal into ink, then release and hold again to convert another. Each ink seal adds 15% to all your damage.

Keep your life seals while learning the attacks. With no life seals left, the next hit kills you even if ink remains. Pressing L spends one ink seal on a flurry; without ink, it does nothing.

For a perfect parry, face the incoming attack and press Shift just before it lands. The perfect window lasts 0.18 seconds, and another press within 0.35 seconds only blocks unless you landed a perfect parry. Three consecutive perfect parries restore one empty seal to life; taking a hit resets that streak.

Prepare for the later phases

The Guardian has 500 health. The second phase begins at 330 health and brings night lighting; the third begins at 165 and turns the arena to volcano dusk. During each 1.45-second repaint, neither fighter can damage the other, making it a safe time to convert life into ink.

Only the sweep, lunge and spin can be blocked or parried. Move out of the ring, beam, leap and ribbon storm; step sideways out of the beam's line. Later phases give you less time between attacks, so keep watching the floor.

If impact flashes make that harder, open O and lower Flashes. The same paused panel includes Brightness, Volume, Music, Effects and Mute. After victory or defeat, use R or controller Start to begin the full fight again.
