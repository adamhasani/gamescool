02 MEME SURGE Patch notes

Patch 1.2

Kitty joins: fighter 10 wins the horde over

Kitty, the comeback cat with the most fans, is fighter 10. Her hearts crush whatever they hit, and the third crush charms it: a charmed enemy fights on your side for a few seconds, and nothing your squad throws can hurt it. Bosses never fall for anyone, but a full crush leaves one smitten. She plays in every mode, co-op included.

General

  • Charmed enemies

    Kitty's fans hunt the horde near her and never touch a fighter. Everyone's shots fly through them, and the horde ignores them. A fan with nobody to fight gets bored and snaps out of it sooner, and for three seconds after that it cannot be charmed again.

    New
  • Smitten bosses

    A boss cannot be charmed. Three crushes leave it smitten instead: for four seconds every fighter's hits on it land 25% harder.

    New

Fighters

Idol · Charmer

Kitty

New

An idol who wins the horde over instead of wiping it out. Her kit comes from her token's own world: the pink bow logo, the pixel hearts and record player on its site, its "cutest takeover in crypto" line, and a comeback from almost nothing. In our test runs (a bot, 36 runs) she won every run, made the most Survival kills of the roster with random upgrades, and cleared District Run in 9:54 with stacked upgrades, next to Zcash and Ansem.

  • Heart Shot

    A pixel heart for 18. Every heart that lands is a crush, and the third crush charms the enemy for five seconds (six and seven as she evolves). A heart that kills is a kill, not a charm.

    New
  • Put a Bow on It

    Toss your bow up to 330 away. It gift-wraps everything it lands on: held for two seconds, two crushes along, so one more heart charms it.

    New
  • Drop the Needle

    A record player for six seconds. Every beat (120 a minute) hits the dance floor around it, every second beat is a crush, and fans on the floor hit twice as fast.

    New
  • Cutest Takeover

    Everything within 420 falls for her at once for seven seconds. A boss is starstruck for a second and smitten for the rest.

    New
  • Comeback

    Once per run, a hit that would take her down leaves her at half health, untouchable for two seconds, and charms everyone around her. A new District Run encounter does not bring it back.

    New
  • Skip

    The quick, untouchable dash every fighter has.

    New

Modes

  • Co-op

    Kitty plays in co-op: her fans fight for the whole squad, and a boss she has smitten takes more damage from everyone. This patch updates the co-op server too, so reload an open Meme Surge tab before you join a room.

    New
